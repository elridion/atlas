# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- In worlds without a map, "Record discoveries" at a cartography table now also updates your atlas,
  and the map key unrolls it: a hand-inked map in the style of a classic fantasy novel, with
  unexplored lands left as blank parchment.
- Every biome has its own pen marks: shaded peaks and hills, round woods in the Meadows, pines in the
  Black Forest, marsh tufts and dead trees in the Swamp, lone trees on the Plains, spires, giant trees
  and mist in the Mistlands, volcanoes and burnt ground in the Ashlands, glaciers and ice floes in
  the Deep North, and two ripple lines along every open coast.
- "Read map" at a cartography table opens the game's own interactive map, where you can place and
  edit pins. Your pins appear on the atlas the next time you record your discoveries.
- The atlas is drawn once per world in the background and kept, so opening it never stalls the game.
